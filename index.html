About

RustyWolf

I’m a developing independent creator who makes games, code experiments, builds and graphics. I’m starting out as a freelancer, so this site labels things honestly: concepts are concepts and finished work is finished work.

What I enjoy making

Game mechanics, interactive experiences, boss fights and abilities, themed builds, and graphics with a cinematic feel.

How I approach commissions

I confirm scope, complexity and timing before I start, and I’d rather tell you what’s realistic than overpromise.

Personal statement
